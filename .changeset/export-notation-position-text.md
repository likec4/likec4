Image exports with `--notation` can place the notation in any corner with `--notation-position` (default `bottom-right`) and choose the labels with `--notation-text`: `short` (text before the first `:`, default), `full` or `kind`. Notation text is no longer cut off, and the panel has a translucent background, so it stays readable in viewers that show transparency as dark.
